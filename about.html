Connecting UNC Students with Meaningful Career Opportunities

Many University of Northern Colorado students struggle to find internships that match their interests, experience, and career goals. Opportunities can be spread across different job boards, company websites, and career events, making the search confusing and very time consuming. At the same time, local employers may have difficulty reaching qualified students who are ready to learn and contribute.

UNC Internship Connect promises to make that process easier by creating one central place where students can discover relevant internships and employers can connect with motivated UNC candidates. By bringing both parties together, the website would help students gain valuable professional experience while helping businesses find emerging talent in their local community.
