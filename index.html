The web is today’s Main Street because it gives businesses and organizations a digital location where people can discover them, learn about their services, and connect from almost anywhere in the world. UNC Internship Connect would use that global reach to help University of Northern Colorado students find internships and help employers reach qualified candidates. Just as a street address helps customers find a physical business, the Domain Name System, or DNS, connects a memorable website address to the server where the site is stored. This creates one accessible digital storefront where students and employers can find information and build professional connections.
